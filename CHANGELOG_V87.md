# FORCE V87

## Penalty Impact rankings

- Renames the Rankings sub-tab from **Penalties** to **Penalty Impact**.
- Entering Penalty Impact now defaults to `Penalty impact ↓`: greatest net benefit from penalties first.
- Adds a dedicated two-way control: **Most benefit first** / **Least benefit first**.
- Keeps unavailable penalty rows at the bottom in either direction.
- Expands the league table with the V86 audit fields: headline blended Penalty Impact, 2026 raw score, recalculated 2025 prior, causal EPA/WPA, event counts, and live-game sample.
- Does **not** change the V86 causal penalty formula, historical recalculation, or three-equivalent-game 2025 carryover.
- Updates the local-server identity and diagnostic handshake to V87 while intentionally retaining the V86 penalty-model cache key because the model itself is unchanged.
