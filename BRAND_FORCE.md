# FORCE identity system

## Name
**FORCE** - Football Objective Rating & Comparative Efficiency.

## Brand grammar
Use FORCE when it names a proprietary output or system:

- FORCE Score
- FORCE Rankings
- FORCEcast
- FORCE only
- FORCE + Market
- FORCE Adaptive

Keep normal football language normal:

- Luck
- Penalties
- Units
- Rating change
- Roster Lab
- Teams
- Games

This avoids gimmicky over-branding.

## Copy standard
1. Plain English.
2. One sentence if one sentence is enough.
3. Explain interpretation before methodology.
4. Put technical detail on Method.
5. Avoid words like “prequential,” “residual,” or “log-odds” on primary pages unless they are the actual data label.


## V48 visual system
- Primary mark: multicolor Vector F (teal / gold / crimson).
- Product chrome: near-black / deep navy with cyan measurement accents.
- Full expansion: **Football Objective Rating & Comparative Efficiency**.
- Rating motif: a restrained 1–100 scale.
- Do not spread the logo's three semantic colors throughout normal UI chrome; reserve them for the mark and selected emphasis.
- PNG export branding must remain self-contained inline SVG.


## V52 Approved PNG Identity

V52 uses the approved FORCE PNG identity as the canonical runtime/export brand asset rather than reconstructing the logo in SVG. SVG mark files remain in the bundle as legacy/fallback references only; topbar, About FORCE, favicon, and PNG export branding consume `assets/force-approved-*.png`.
