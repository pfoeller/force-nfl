# FORCE V73

## Unit matchup-edge donuts
- Adds presentation-only matchup-edge donuts for QB vs Coverage, Receivers vs Coverage, OL vs Pass Rush, and RB vs Run Defense.
- Raw unit grades remain on FORCE's red/yellow/green 0–100 scale.
- Donut slices use team colors and the advantaged team's logo appears in the center.
- Relative share uses a conservative symmetric transform of unit-grade differential: 50/50 at equal grades, soft-capped near 85/15 for extreme mismatches.
- Historical unit snapshots are insufficient for a leakage-safe fitted calibration, so these shares do not alter FORCEcast in V73.
