# FORCE V66

- Adds **Divisions** and **Playoff Picture** top-level tabs.
- Runs 5,000 deterministic remaining-season simulations from current FORCEcast game win probabilities.
- Divisions show current record/FORCE, projected record, most common final division finish, and division-title probability.
- Playoff Picture shows current record/FORCE, projected record, most common projected seed, playoff probability, division probability, and first-round-bye probability.
- Equal simulated records use current FORCE as a transparent tiebreak proxy rather than claiming full NFL tiebreak reproduction.
