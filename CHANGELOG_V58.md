# FORCE V58

## Export copy minimization
- PNG exports now suppress explanatory/supporting copy so exported matchup, ratings, and related boards emphasize the calculated ratings themselves.
- Exported pages remove row-level helper text (`small` / `.sub`) plus descriptive notes such as matchup footnotes, market-detail blurbs, forecast audit strips, and similar explanatory copy.
- On-site views remain unchanged; the simplification is export-only so site visitors still get the full context and explanations inside the app.
