# FORCE identity trial

V9 intentionally treats **FORCE** as the full product name, not an engine beneath another brand.

## Why it works
- Football-native word.
- Easy to say and remember.
- Covers ratings, forecasts, matchup analysis, and roster scenarios.
- Creates useful proprietary labels without renaming ordinary football concepts.

## In-app language
- FORCE Score
- FORCE Rankings
- FORCEcast
- FORCE only
- FORCE + Market
- FORCE Adaptive

## What stays plain
Luck, Penalties, Units, Games, Teams, Rating change, and Roster Lab.

This is product/search screening, not legal trademark or domain clearance.
