# FORCE V71 - Game Flow research

- Adds a **Game Flow · Research** panel to matchup pages with Q1, halftime, Q3 and final expected cumulative scores.
- Uses the existing FORCEcast final score and league-average 2025 quarter scoring shape; does not alter FORCEcast probability, line, score or headline FORCE rating.
- Adds descriptive 2025 team quarter profiles, explicitly not used as predictive inputs.
- Adds a research report documenting that prior-year team-specific quarter timing worsened next-year quarter-share RMSE by 41.6% versus the league-average baseline, so the raw quarter-timing feature was rejected for predictive use.
- Establishes the promotion gate for future opponent-adjusted trailing/leading EPA features.
